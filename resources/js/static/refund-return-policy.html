1.Overview

We want you to feel sure about every order. This policy explains which items you can return, how long you have, and when your money comes back. It applies to all orders placed on MediMart, on the website, in the app and by phone.

Medicines are health products, so some items follow stricter rules than beauty or lifestyle products. These are marked clearly in the table below.

2.What you can return

Return windows start on the day your order is delivered.

3.Wrong, damaged or expired items

If your order arrives with a problem, contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We treat these as our responsibility and arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

  • Wrong item: a different product, strength or quantity from what you ordered.
  • Damaged item: a broken seal, leaking or crushed pack, or a device that is cracked or does not switch on.
  • Expired or short-dated item: already expired, or expiring within 3 months of delivery, unless the product page states otherwise.

Do not use or take the item

If you think a medicine is wrong or damaged, keep it in its packaging and do not use it. We will ask for the pack, strip or bottle when we collect it.

4.How to request a return

  1. 1

    Start your request

    Go to Orders, choose the order and select Return item. You can also call 09610-016778 or message us on WhatsApp.

  2. 2

    Tell us what happened

    Choose a reason and upload clear photos. For wrong or damaged items, include the pack and the batch and expiry label.

  3. 3

    We review it

    We reply within 1 working day by SMS and in the app. If more detail is needed, our team will call you.

  4. 4

    Hand over the item

    Pack the item in its original packaging. Our rider collects it from your delivery address.

  5. 5

    Get your refund

    We inspect the item and, once approved, send your refund by the method in the next section.

6.Cancelling an order

  • Before dispatch: cancel from Orders or by phone for a full refund.
  • After dispatch: you can refuse the parcel at the door, or return it under this policy once it arrives.
  • Prescription orders: you can cancel until our pharmacist confirms the prescription.

7.Pickup and delivery charges

  • When the mistake is ours, pickup is free and we refund the delivery charge as well.
  • For change-of-mind returns of eligible items, the delivery charge is not refunded. If a return pickup fee applies, we show it before you confirm.
  • Outside Dhaka, returns are collected through a courier and may take longer to reach us.
