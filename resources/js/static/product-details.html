Description

Skin1004 Centella Sun Serum is a daily sunscreen with a serum-like texture. It gives SPF50+ PA++++ protection and spreads thinly, so it works as the last step of a morning routine without a heavy feel.

It is made with Centella Asiatica extract from Madagascar, an ingredient widely used in Korean skincare for calming skin. Use it on the face and neck every morning, and on any exposed skin when you are outdoors.

How to use

  1. 1

    Finish your usual skincare. Sunscreen goes on last, after moisturiser.

  2. 2

    Take about half a teaspoon for the face and neck.

  3. 3

    Dot it over your face and blend evenly. Wait a minute before makeup.

  4. 4

    Reapply every 2 hours when outdoors, and after sweating or swimming.

Precautions

  • For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes; rinse with water if it happens.
  • Stop using it and see a doctor if redness or irritation develops.
  • Store below 30°C, away from direct sunlight, and keep out of reach of children.
