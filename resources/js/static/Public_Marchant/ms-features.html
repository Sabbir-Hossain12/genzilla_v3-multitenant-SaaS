Use cases

Built for physical and digital goods alike

Sell Physical Goods

From handmade jewelry to bulk wholesale — manage stock, shipping, and fulfillment without leaving your dashboard.

  • Inventory management — track stock across variants, locations, and low-stock alerts.
  • Live shipping rates — calculate accurate rates at checkout from major couriers.
  • Order fulfillment tracking — print labels and share tracking numbers automatically.
  • Multi-warehouse support — route orders to the nearest fulfillment location.
  • Returns management — self-serve return requests with configurable policies.

Sell Digital Goods

Courses, ebooks, software licenses, or memberships — deliver instantly with zero manual work.

  • Instant download delivery — secure, expiring links sent the moment payment clears.
  • License key generation — auto-generate and track unique keys per sale.
  • Protected streaming — host video/audio content with access controls, not raw file links.
  • Subscriptions & memberships — recurring billing with automatic access renewal.
  • Automatic email delivery — receipts and files sent together, fully customizable.
