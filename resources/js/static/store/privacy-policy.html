MediMart Ltd. (“MediMart”, “we”, “us”) runs the MediMart website and apps, an online pharmacy for medicine, beauty and healthcare products in Bangladesh. We decide how the personal information described in this policy is used.

3.How we use your information

  • Process, deliver and support your orders.
  • Have a licensed pharmacist verify prescriptions before medicines are dispensed.
  • Send order updates by SMS, in the app or by email.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, and protect your account.
  • Improve our products, search and delivery times.
  • Send offers and health tips, only if you agree. You can opt out at any time.
  • Meet legal, tax and pharmacy regulation requirements.

4.Prescriptions and health information

Prescriptions and medicine orders can reveal sensitive details about your health, so we handle them with extra care.

How we protect health information

Only our pharmacists, authorised support staff and the licensed pharmacy filling your order can see your prescriptions. We do not use them for advertising.

  • We use prescriptions only to verify and fulfil your order.
  • You can delete uploaded prescriptions from your account. Where pharmacy regulations require us to keep a dispensing record, we keep only that record.
  • We never share health information with advertisers.

5.Who we share it with

We share information only when it is needed to serve you or required by law.

  • Delivery partners: your name, address, phone number and the parcel they carry.
  • Payment providers: bKash, Nagad, Rocket, card networks and banks, to take payment and issue refunds.
  • Licensed pharmacies and sellers: the details needed to fill and ship your order.
  • Doctors and labs: details for consultations and tests you book through MediMart.
  • Service providers: SMS, hosting and analytics companies working for us under contract.
  • Authorities: when a court, regulator or law enforcement body lawfully requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

7.How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the reasons above.

  • Account information is kept while your account is active and removed after you delete it.
  • Order and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Prescription and dispensing records are kept for as long as pharmacy regulations require.
  • Support and device data is kept for a shorter period, then deleted or anonymised.

8.Keeping your information safe

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), OTP login and role-based access so staff see only what their job needs. No system is completely secure, so please protect your account too.

Never share your OTP

MediMart staff will never ask you for your OTP or password, by phone, WhatsApp or SMS.

9.Your choices and rights

  • See and download the information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your account and data. Some records must be kept where the law requires it.
  • Stop marketing messages using the unsubscribe link or Account settings.
  • Withdraw your consent for optional uses of your information.

To use any of these, email privacy@medimart.com.bd or call 09610-016778. We reply within 15 working days.

10.Children

MediMart is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please order with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children without a guardian’s consent.

11.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. For important changes, we will tell you in the app or by SMS before they take effect. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
